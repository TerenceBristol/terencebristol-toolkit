# My Thoughts on Ali Abdaal's Claude Video Editing Experiment

Video: https://youtu.be/f0dv37Rh-UQ

Everything from this video, free.

## Items

- [/interview-me](../skills/interview-me/): Claude looks things up, asks you one question at a time with its pick, then agrees the plan with you before any work starts.
- [/improve](../skills/improve/): after every video (or any conversation), Claude folds your feedback back into its skills, so the next edit starts closer to done.

## Prompts from the video

Before Claude starts on a task, tell your Claude:

```
interview me first and ask me any questions you have
```
