## Prompts from the video

While you record (step 1), say this out loud before you redo a line:

```
cut that
```

When your Claude makes the rough cut (step 2), tell your Claude:

```
ask if it can drive your editor
```

After you polish the rough cut (step 3), tell your Claude:

```
Hey, I'm done polishing the timeline. Can you watch it again?
```

When your Claude makes the stills (step 4), tell your Claude:

```
you'll animate these stills later
```

When the graphics are animated (step 5), tell your Claude:

```
put the motion graphics in the timeline
```
