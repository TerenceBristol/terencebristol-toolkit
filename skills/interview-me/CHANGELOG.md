# Changelog

All notable changes to the `/interview-me` skill are documented here.

Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-22

### Changed
- A direct question in your brief is answered after the quick check that settles it, instead of a provisional guess; "not checked yet" only when that check is long.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added
- First public version: looks things up before asking, asks one question at a time with a recommendation, and ends with an agreed plan.
- A review variant for going through feedback, comments or findings item by item.
